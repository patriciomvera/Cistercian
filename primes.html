The Anatomy of the Primes

Every prime below ten thousand fits in a single Cistercian glyph. Here you can hear the primes as a score, overlay all 1,229 glyphs into one ghost, and check which patterns belong to the primes and which belong to the notation itself.

What is really prime here? Almost all of the signal lives in the units quadrant. Every prime above 5 ends in 1, 3, 7 or 9, and that leaves a clear mark on the strokes. The other three quadrants look almost exactly like ordinary integers. Big swings such as the notch at 6,000 come from how the digits are drawn, not from the primes. Every view below can compare against all integers 1–9,999, so you can check this for yourself.
